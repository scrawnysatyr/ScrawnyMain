Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how scrawny.site and its subdomains (including boards.scrawny.site and pdf.scrawny.site) handle information when you visit or use our web applications.

1. Local & Client-Side Processing

Our web tools are designed to run directly in your browser without requiring user accounts or login. Files processed in ScrawnyPDF stay on your device, and multiplayer sessions on ScrawnyBoards use peer-to-peer WebRTC connections.

2. Advertising & Google AdSense

We use Google AdSense to serve advertisements on scrawny.site and its subdomains. Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings.

3. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can reach out via GitHub at github.com/scrawnysatyr.
